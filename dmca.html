DMCA & Copyright Policy

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

⚖️ 1. DMCA Copyright Policy Overview

AstroMods is a public online service provider that lets creators host user-generated content, mod files, and script assets. We take copyright infringements very seriously and fully comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

If you are a copyright owner or an official representative, and you believe that any user-uploaded file on AstroMods infringes upon your copyright, you can request an expedited takedown.

📝 2. Instructions to File a Takedown Notice

To report an infringement, you must submit a written copyright takedown notice to our email. Your notice must include:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
  • Detailed identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Direct URL or links pointing to the specific mod file hosted on AstroMods that you want removed.
  • Your contact information including fullname, email address, physical address, and phone number.
  • A statement that you have a "good faith belief" that the use of the material is not authorized.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that the information in your notice is accurate.

↩️ 3. Counter-Notification Procedure

If your uploaded mod or script has been wrongfully removed due to a copyright filing, you can send a Counter-Notification to restore your content.

Upon receiving a valid counter-notice, we will forward it to the original reporting party. If they do not initiate legal action within 10-14 business days, the mod will be fully restored.

🚫 4. Repeat Violations

In accordance with the DMCA, AstroMods enforces a strict **Repeat Infringer Policy**.

Any registered uploader who receives multiple valid copyright strikes or has files removed repeatedly due to infringement reports will face permanent account termination and IP bans from our networks.
